### FRAME 28: DETACHED INTIMACY
### THE MANTRA-CODE:
A raw, claustrophobic bedroom documentary photograph,  
Grainy 35mm analog film style with a moody domestic lighting.  
A man and a woman are lying flat on a bed in a dark room,  
Positioned back-to-back with a stark physical gap between them.  
The space is swallowed by pitch-black, heavy darkness.  

Yet both of their faces are sharply illuminated from below  
By the intense, cold blue-white light of their smartphones.  
Their glazed, empty eyes are staring blankly into the screens,  
Completely swallowed by separate digital vortexes.  

High contrast, deep moody shadows, raw silver halide noise.  
Fujichrome film grading with muted realistic flesh tones  
Vs the blinding, cold artificial digital glow.  
An emotional narrative of total alienation between human beings,  
No 3D-render smoothness, only raw non-digital texture.
