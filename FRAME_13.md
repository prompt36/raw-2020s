### FRAME 13: THE VIRTUAL DESK
### THE MANTRA-CODE:
A raw, high-contrast documentary photograph,  
35mm analog film texture with a fine grain, cinematic lighting.  
A vast, ultra-modern corporate open-space office,  
Towering glass windows overlooking a blurred megacity at twilight.  
Long rows of neat white desks and empty ergonomic chairs  
Fading into a heavy background perspective.  

Most computer screens are left turned on, casting an eerie blue light,  
Displaying unread charts, graphs, and lines of running code.  
In the absolute middle of this vast, silent, desolated room,  
A single lonely employee sits at their desk, head low, isolated.  

Moody corporate atmosphere, deep heavy shadows, and sharp contrast.  
Fujichrome film color palette with an authentic non-digital look,  
Capturing the ghost ship emptiness of remote work reality,  
No 3D-render smoothness, only raw texture.
