### FRAME 3: THE GREEN CURRENT
### THE MANTRA-CODE:

A stark, moody nocturnal landscape photograph,  
Grainy 35mm analog film style with a fine silver halide noise.  
A single modern EV charging station stands illuminated,  
Under a lone, harsh streetlight on an empty highway.  
Thick, heavy night fog and damp mist enveloping the scene,  
Blurring the cold, distant dark silhouettes of trees.  

The station's small digital display and LED indicators  
Cast a cold green and sharp white glow onto wet asphalt.  
Deep heavy shadows and a striking graphical contrast,  
Muted real-world tones shifting into a dark atmosphere.  
Rich Kodachrome film grading with deep ink-black shadows.  

An eerie, silent transition of industrial civilization,  
No 3D-render smoothness, only raw analog texture,  
The cold breath of the current on a dark road.
