### FRAME 23: THE LITHIUM GORGE
### THE MANTRA-CODE:

A raw, high-contrast environmental documentary photograph,  
Grainy 35mm analog film style with a moody cinematic look.  
A vast, dry desert canyon completely filled and choked  
With mountains of corroded lithium batteries and old phones.  
The toxic tech waste extends endlessly to the distant horizon,  
Leaving chemical stains on the cracked, poisoned earth.  

In the foreground, a single lonely vulture is perched  
On a massive pile of weathered, broken circuit boards,  
Looking away into the bleak, hazy distance.  
Overcast, flat, depressing daylight casting deep shadows.  
Muted realistic tones and a raw silver halide noise texture.  

Fujichrome film grading capturing the heavy ecological footprint,  
The hidden price of clean energy buried far from consumer eyes,  
No 3D-render smoothness, only raw non-digital texture.
