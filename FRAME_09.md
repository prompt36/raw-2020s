### FRAME 9: THE PICKUP POINT
### THE MANTRA-CODE:

A raw, candid 35mm documentary photograph.  
A cramped, brightly lit parcel pickup point  
In a gritty, cold basement, replacing retail shops.  
High, messy shelves crammed with many boxes  
And heavily taped commercial plastic parcels.  

A tired worker behind a cheap laminate counter  
Holds a glowing red laser barcode scanner.  
A fabric curtain conceals a small fitting booth.  
An exhausted customer tearing open a mailer bag  
Under buzzing, bleak and cold white LEDs.  
Discarded packaging tape and crumpled wrapping  
Strewn on the dirty, scuffed linoleum floor.  

Bleak fluorescent glare, deep moody contrast,  
Muted real tones, realistic mundane textures.  
Fujichrome film style with fine silver halide noise.  
A cynical snapshot of instant consumer dopamine  
And a forgotten, completely lost human connection.  
No 3D-render smoothness, only raw realism.
