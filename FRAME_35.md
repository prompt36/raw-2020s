### FRAME 35: THE EYE OF THE WITNESS
### THE MANTRA-CODE:

A raw, extreme macro documentary portrait,  
Grainy 35mm analog film style with a deeply intense look.  
A tight close-up shot focusing completely on a sharp human eye,  
Revealing weathered details of the iris and raw skin texture.  
Reflected clear within the dark, wet cornea  
Is a wide-angle camera lens pointing directly back at viewer.  

The gaze is exhausted, naked, yet intensely and piercingly sharp.  
Harsh, high-contrast graphical lighting carving deep creases,  
And a subtle hint of a single tear on the skin.  
Moody shadows, rich silver halide noise, distinct film grading.  

Muted realistic skin tones capturing the psychological weight,  
The pen-ultimate frame of the chronicle before the shutter closes,  
No 3D-render smoothness, only raw atmospheric texture.
