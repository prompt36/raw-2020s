### FRAME 20: THE VIRTUAL SHADOW
### THE MANTRA-CODE:

A raw, high-contrast documentary street photograph,  
Grainy 35mm analog film style with an authentic reportage look.  
A sleek, minimalist glass storefront of a luxury digital boutique.  
Inside the window, bright holographic displays showcase  
Virtual clothes and glowing digital sneakers for metaverses.  

On the dark, cold pavement directly below the glossy window,  
A homeless person sleeps on flat, crumpled cardboard boxes,  
Wrapped tightly in a worn, weathered winter coat.  
The blinding neon reflections from virtual luxury ads clash  
With the wet, dark, light-reflective texture of the asphalt.  

Deep heavy shadows, high graphical contrast, silver halide noise.  
Fujichrome film grading capturing the absurd economic divide,  
No 3D-render smoothness, only raw atmospheric texture.
