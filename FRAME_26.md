### FRAME 26: THE SILICON ALTAR
### THE MANTRA-CODE:

A raw, high-contrast interior documentary photograph,  
Grainy 35mm analog film style with a moody cinematic lighting.  
Inside a vast, abandoned church with peeling paint,  
Cracked brickwork, and fading, ghost-like fresco walls.  
A massive crypto-mining farm completely replaces the altar.  

Towering rows of metallic racks are filled with graphics cards,  
Humming and casting a chaotic, flickering neon grid light,  
Eerie shades of green, cold blue, and violet onto dark masonry.  
A tangled web of black wires litters the dusty stone floor.  
Deep heavy shadows, sharp graphical contrast, silver halide noise.  

Fujichrome film grading capturing a new global religion,  
Trading spiritual eternity for cold mining of digital currency,  
No 3D-render smoothness, only raw atmospheric decay.
