### FRAME 18: THE REFUGEE SHADOW
### THE MANTRA-CODE:
A raw, wide-angle documentary photograph,  
Cinematic 35mm film grain texture, authentic reportage look.  
In the immediate foreground, a displaced refugee family  
Stands outside fragile canvas tents in a mud-covered field.  
Their faces are weathered, looking toward the far horizon.  

In the blurry distance, an ultra-modern wealthy megacity  
Skyline rises up, glowing faintly under a cold twilight sky.  
Flat depressing evening daylight, high contrast, heavy shadows.  
Fujichrome film grading, raw silver halide noise, muted colors.  

Capturing the sharp social divide and human vulnerability,  
The final frame where geopolitics hurts the flesh,  
No 3D-render smoothness, only raw atmospheric realism.
