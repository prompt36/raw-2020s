### FRAME 2: POLAR PROXY
### THE MANTRA-CODE:

A raw, candid documentary street photograph,  
Grainy 35mm analog film style with an authentic look.  
An autonomous delivery robot rolls down a wet sidewalk,  
Lost in a thick morning mist and dim, cold streetlights.  
The robot's body is scratched and splattered with mud.  

Right next to the machine, a stray dog is standing,  
Sniffing the mechanical frame in confusion and curiosity.  
Harsh graphical lighting and deep moody shadows on asphalt.  
Muted realistic colors with a Fujichrome film grading,  
Fine silver halide noise and strong cinematic contrast.  

A quiet clash between primal life and cybernetic tech.  
Defocused industrial background, atmospheric distortion.  
No 3D-render smoothness, only raw texture.
