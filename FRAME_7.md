### FRAME 7: THE BEAUTY CONVEYOR
### THE MANTRA-CODE:

A raw, candid documentary photograph,  
Grainy 35mm analog film style with harsh clinical lighting.  
In a cold, minimalist corridor of a plastic surgery clinic,  
Three young women sit side by side on medical chairs.  
Their heads and faces are heavily wrapped in surgical gauze  
And medical bandages after operations, hiding identities.  

All three women have their heads tilted down, absorbed,  
Staring into the bright screens of their smartphones.  
Cold white and blue light from screens dimly illuminates  
The bloody and bandaged parts of their faces.  
High contrast, deep heavy shadows, moody atmospheric realism,  
Muted clothing tones clashing with blinding digital screens.  

Fine silver halide noise and a distinct Fujichrome film grading.  
Capturing the eerie standardization of modern identity,  
No 3D-render smoothness, only raw analog texture.
