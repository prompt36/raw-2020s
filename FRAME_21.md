### FRAME 21: THE LIKE FACTORY
### THE MANTRA-CODE:

A raw, high-contrast interior documentary photograph,  
Grainy 35mm analog film style with a cinematic claustrophobic framing.  
Inside a dark, narrow, dusty semi-basement room in Asia,  
Industrial metal racks are filled with thousands of smartphones.  
Screens stacked in rows, automatically and rapidly scrolling  
Through social media interfaces and digital popularity metrics.  

The displays cast a chaotic, flickering matrix of neon light.  
In the center of this cyber-farm, an exhausted Asian man  
Sits on a cheap chair, his face dimly lit by the blinding grid.  
Deep moody shadows, sharp graphical contrast, silver halide noise.  

Fujichrome film grading capturing machinery behind digital success,  
The hidden factory where false popularity is manufactured,  
No 3D-render smoothness, only raw analog texture.
