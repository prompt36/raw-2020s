### FRAME 14: THE AUTOMATED CAFE
### THE MANTRA-CODE:

A raw, candid documentary photograph,  
Grainy 35mm analog film style with a fine silver halide noise.  
A modern, minimalist coffee shop with sleek counter designs,  
Completely devoid of human workers behind the bar.  
In the center, a high-tech robotic manipulator arm  
Is precisely pouring coffee into a single paper cup.  

The scene is lit by harsh, cold LED lighting mixed  
With warm pendant lamps, creating deep moody shadows.  
In the background, a single lonely customer is standing,  
Waiting for their order while staring blankly into a screen.  

Fujichrome film grading with muted realistic colors,  
Capturing the quiet erosion of casual human touch,  
No 3D-render smoothness, only raw non-digital texture.
