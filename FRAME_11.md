### FRAME 11: THE TERMINAL ASIA
### THE MANTRA-CODE:

A cinematic 35mm documentary photograph,  
A heavy security checkpoint inside a grand government hall  
In an Asian megacity. Arched glass windows reveal  
A bright, sunny, free city outside, sharply contrasting  
With oppressive, cold fluorescent shadows indoors.  
Stern uniformed officers rigidly controlling bottleneck exits.  

Three distinct human interactions:  
A weary traveler placing a bag on an X-ray conveyor  
While looking up at an officer pointing aggressively,  
A young woman holding up her glowing smartphone,  
Screen coldly illuminating an inspector's stoic face,  
Another citizen silently escorted aside, detained from crowd.  

Tense, heavy bureaucratic anxiety, freedom under strict control.  
Analog film style, Kodachrome grading, fine silver halide noise,  
No 3D-render smoothness, raw atmospheric realism.
