### FRAME 8: THE CAPTIVE RUNNER
### THE MANTRA-CODE:

A raw, claustrophobic indoor documentary photograph,  
Grainy 35mm analog film style, moody domestic lighting.  
A lonely human is running on a modern treadmill,  
Cramped inside a tiny, suffocatingly cluttered room.  
A heavy VR headset is strapped tightly to their head,  
Hiding their face, broadcasting a simulated autumn forest.  

Through the small, dust-covered window of the room,  
Bleak daylight reveals the unyielding gray concrete facade  
Of a massive neighboring building just a few meters away.  
Deep moody shadows and a high graphical contrast,  
Muted real-world colors, raw silver halide noise.  

Fujichrome film grading, tense and melancholic atmosphere.  
A striking snapshot of synthetic freedom inside a concrete cage,  
No 3D-render smoothness, only raw atmospheric texture.
