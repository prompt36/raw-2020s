### FRAME 6: MANUFACTURED HARMONY
### THE MANTRA-CODE:

A raw, candid urban photograph,  
Grainy 35mm analog film style with heavy silver halide noise.  
A massive, blindingly bright digital billboard  
Mounted on the weathered concrete wall of a gray high-rise.  
On the screen — a beautiful woman in a perfect yoga pose  
Beneath a fake neon sun, promoting toxic optimism.  

Below, the winter street is drowning in melting gray slush,  
Dirty snow and puddles bleeding with pink-orange reflections.  
A lonely city bus rolls blindly through the overcast daylight.  
A cynical clashing of beautiful manufactured illusions  
And the raw, heavy weight of modern digital burnout.  

Fujichrome film style, deep cinematic contrast,  
The fragile vulnerability of a crumbling world.  
No 3D-render smoothness, only raw texture.
