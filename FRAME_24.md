### FRAME 24: THE TECH REEF
### THE MANTRA-CODE:

A raw, high-contrast environmental documentary photograph,  
Grainy 35mm analog film style with an authentic cinematic look.  
A wide shot of a massive, dense island of plastic pollution,  
Floating heavily on the surface of the dark ocean waves.  
The debris consists of computer keyboards, tangled wires,  
And discarded electronic parts stretching to the horizon.  

In the absolute center, a single sea bird is caught,  
Struggling to break through techno-waste toward the sun.  
Harsh natural daylight and bright sun reflections on wet plastic.  
Deep graphical contrast and a raw silver halide noise texture.  

Fujichrome film grading capturing our permanent footprint,  
The physical residue of a lifestyle suffocating ecosystems,  
No 3D-render smoothness, only raw atmospheric realism.
