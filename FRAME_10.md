### FRAME 10: METRO BLUE
### THE MANTRA-CODE:

A raw, candid documentary street photograph inside a subway car,  
Grainy 35mm analog film style with an authentic reportage look.  
A crowded underground metro train packed tightly with people,  
Standing shoulder to shoulder in absolute physical proximity.  

The train interior is dark and gloomy,  
Yet every single person's face is sharply illuminated  
From below by the intense, cold blue-white light of screens.  
All passengers have identical glazed, empty, hypnotic expressions,  
Eyes locked onto displays, completely dead and isolated.  

Harsh graphical contrast and deep moody shadows,  
Muted real-world tones vs the blinding artificial screen glow.  
Fujichrome film grading with a fine silver halide noise,  
A heavy, claustrophobic and tense atmosphere,  
No 3D-render smoothness, only raw non-digital texture.
