### FRAME 15: THE WEBCAM DESPATCH
### THE MANTRA-CODE:

A raw, high-contrast interior documentary photograph,  
Grainy 35mm analog film style with a fine silver halide noise.  
In an old school classroom with a green chalkboard,  
An older, tired female teacher stands talking passionately.  
Gesturing, she looks directly into a webcam on a tripod.  

Rows of empty wooden desks stretch in the foreground,  
Completely deserted, silent, and empty.  
Soft natural daylight falls through the large windows,  
Casting long, melancholic shadows across dusty floorboards.  

Deep contrast, muted realistic colors, heavy narrative.  
Fujichrome film grading capturing desolation of digital learning.  
The quiet disappearance of physical socialization,  
No 3D-render smoothness, only raw atmospheric texture.
