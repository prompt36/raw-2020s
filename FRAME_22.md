### FRAME 22: THE CONSUMER MATRIX
### THE MANTRA-CODE:

A raw, high-contrast industrial documentary photograph,  
Grainy 35mm analog film style with an authentic cinematic look.  
The interior of a massive, monolithic automated warehouse.  
Towering, endless rows of metal shelves stacked with boxes,  
Creating an oppressive, symmetrical perspective fading into shadows.  

High-tech robotic manipulator arms and sorting machinery  
Slide along rails, lit by harsh overhead fluorescent lamps  
And tiny blinking red laser sensors. No humans in sight.  
Deep moody shadows, cold mechanical light, high contrast.  

Fujichrome film grading with a raw silver halide noise texture,  
Capturing the faceless conveyor belt of immediate cravings,  
No 3D-render smoothness, only raw industrial texture.
