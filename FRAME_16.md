### FRAME 16: DIGITAL EXCLUSION
### THE MANTRA-CODE:
A raw, candid documentary street photograph,  
Grainy 35mm analog film style with a gritty reportage look.  
An elderly wrinkled woman stands completely helpless  
In front of the glass entrance of a public building.  
A terminal displays a massive, complex geometric QR code,  
Marking the only absolute way to authorize and enter inside.  

The woman looks confused and paralyzed by technology,  
Clutching an old-fashioned push-button mobile phone.  
Flat overcast daylight casting realistic, soft shadows.  
Deep contrast, muted real colors, silver halide noise.  

Fujichrome film grading capturing the heavy social divide.  
A poignant snapshot of digital age segregation,  
No 3D-render smoothness, only raw non-digital texture.
