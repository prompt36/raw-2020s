### FRAME 5: ECO-HYPOCRISY
### THE MANTRA-CODE:

A raw, wide-angle documentary street photograph,  
Grainy 35mm analog film style with a gritty reportage look.  
A crowd of trendy young people at an eco-protest rally  
Standing in a sunlit square with smartphones in hands.  
Holding neatly printed cardboard signs with slogans,  
Their eyes locked onto screens instead of the world.  

In the foreground, the concrete is heavily cluttered,  
Strewn with single-use plastic cups, crushed disposable vapes,  
And littered brand-name wrappers left by the activists.  
Harsh natural daylight casting deep, unforgiving shadows.  
Real-world dirt, trash texture, and organic imperfections.  

Fujichrome film grading with fine silver halide noise.  
A cynical snapshot of performative awareness,  
No 3D-render smoothness, only raw atmospheric realism.
