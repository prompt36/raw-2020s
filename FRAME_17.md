### FRAME 17: THE LOCKED HORIZON
### THE MANTRA-CODE:

A raw, macro documentary still-life photograph,  
Grainy 35mm analog film style with a moody cinematic look.  
A close-up shot of an old passport lying on a wooden desk,  
Its paper cover faded, pages thick with ink border stamps.  
Resting on the open passport is a sleek plastic ID card  
With a sterile, glowing biometric microchip embedded.  

In the shallow background, a glowing computer monitor  
Displays a world map crisscrossed with harsh red lines,  
Marking restricted boundaries and closed flight zones.  
Moody ambient room lighting, high contrast, heavy shadows.  
Fujichrome film grading, raw silver halide noise texture.  

Capturing the fragmented reality of modern geopolitics,  
Frozen borders and electronic locks on human travel,  
No 3D-render smoothness, only raw analog texture.
